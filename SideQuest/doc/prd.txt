Product Requirements Document (PRD)
Version 1.0 | 14 Oct 2026 | Status: draft | Working name: HackRadar

1. Summary
A web app (installable as a PWA) that gathers hackathons from many platforms and colleges into one searchable,
filterable list, highlights what is closing soon, and sends alerts so students never miss a registration deadline.

2. Problem statement
• Hackathons are spread across many sites (Unstop, Devfolio, Devpost, MLH, Hack2skill, college pages and
social posts).

• Registration windows are short and easy to miss; many events open and close quietly.
• College and local hackathons often appear on no major platform at all.
• Existing sites filter poorly for what a student cares about: eligibility, city, team size, prize and theme together.

3. Vision, goals and non-goals

7. Functional requirements
• FR-1 The system shall refresh listings from every configured source at least every 6 hours.
• FR-2 Each listing shall store title, organiser, mode, location, event dates, registration deadline, prize, team size,
eligibility, tags and source link.

• FR-3 Duplicate listings from different sources shall appear once, with links to all sources.
• FR-4 Users shall filter by any combination of mode, city, status, tag, prize minimum and team size.
• FR-5 Status (open, closing soon, closed) shall be computed from dates, with closing soon meaning within 72
hours.

• FR-6 Closed hackathons shall be hidden from the default view but remain reachable by link.

11. Risks and assumptions
• Sources may restrict scraping or change formats; mitigated by adapters, multiple sources and user
submissions.

• Free-tier limits could throttle growth; mitigated by caching and monitoring.
• Assumes students prefer a single curated feed over checking each platform.
• Assumes enough public data exists per listing to power filters (prize and team size are sometimes missing).
