• A failed source is retried once, then skipped; the other sources still complete.
• Rows missing a title, link or any date are dropped and counted in the log.
• Enrichment runs only for new or changed rows, so AI usage stays inside the free tier.
• Listings not seen for 14 days are hidden, not deleted.

• Closing-soon alerts fire at 48 hours and 24 hours before the registration deadline.
• The unique key (user, hackathon, kind) in notifications_log stops repeat messages if a job reruns.
• A user can mute alerts from the Telegram bot with /mute.

• Reject path: admin picks a reason, status becomes rejected, and the submitter is notified.
• The form is rate limited and has a hidden honeypot field to catch bots.
• Approved user-submitted events are labelled so people know the source.
