The problem

Hackathons are everywhere. Finding the right one shouldn't take five tabs.

Scattered everywhere

Unstop, Devfolio, Devpost, college pages and social posts. One weekend, five places to check.

Deadlines vanish

Registration windows are short and many close without a reminder. You find out after it's over.

Campus events stay hidden

College and local hackathons often never reach the big platforms, so nearby students miss them.

How it works

Three steps. Zero tab hopping.

1

We collect

Listings are pulled from multiple sources every few hours and merged into one clean format.

2

Mode, city, prize, team size, theme and eligibility. Closing soon always comes first.

3

You get alerted

Telegram or email before registration closes. Then register on the original site.
